07 / USEFUL TO KNOW

A few questions, answered.

Can I rent a furnished apartment?

Yes. Furnished and unfurnished rates are shown for the apartments at 4 Van Reenen Street and 21 Roode Street. Ask about availability and what is included before deciding.

Where can I stay overnight?

Home Away From Home is at 21 Roode Street, Middelburg, Eastern Cape. The listed rate is R500 per night. Enquire about your preferred dates and the arrangements for your stay.

Do the apartment counts show current vacancies?

No. The counts describe the apartments at each property. Please enquire to confirm which units are available and when you can move in.

How do I arrange a viewing or check rental terms?

Choose an apartment and use its enquiry button. Include your preferred viewing or move-in date and ask about the deposit, utilities and lease terms so these can be confirmed.
